More Than A Collection.
A Family Tradition.

Nestled opposite the revered Sri Kanika Parmeshwari Temple along the vibrant Gandhi Bazar Main Road, Pearl Collection has stood as an indelible cornerstone of family celebrations across Malnad and Central Karnataka.

We bring generations together beneath one roof—from grandmother's cherished Kanjivaram and Banarasi weaves, to bridal lehengas crafted for modern grand entrances, tailored bandhgalas for the groom, and festive silhouettes designed for youthful celebrations. Every thread honors provenance; every consultation is tailored with unhurried reverence.
